# Koiros 2.0

**Lee Ostadi’s evolving computational wallpaper — now one shared world.**

Download **Koiros-2.0.apk**, install it on Android 8.0 or newer, open Koiros,
and choose **Set wallpaper**. Live-wallpaper support is required. Root and
Ostadix are not required. Upgrading from 1.8 or from Koi / Life keeps your
saved controls; the new **Merging**, **Respond to me**, and **Know my day** switches start on.

## What is new

- **One world.** Every form swims in the same Game of Life world and its
  measured currents, senses the life around it, and seeds new life in its wake.
- **Merging and dividing.** Forms that meet fuse into one larger body with a
  single rhythm, rotation, colour, and form, then divide again along a new axis,
  each keeping part of what it shared.
- **Colour in the water.** Hue painted into the world travels with living
  patterns and tints the forms that later swim through it.
- **Brighter light.** Additive neon blending, glow, hue-true auras, threads of
  light, and flashes at fusion and division.
- **A companion.** Forms come to look when you tap and welcome you back;
  with usage access they take on the colours of the apps you used. Nothing
  leaves the phone: Koiros has no internet permission.
- **Calmer on power.** 24 fps once you stop touching the screen (20 fps in
  Battery Saver or on a low battery), with a matching display-rate request.

## Verification

Ten dependency-free simulation suites run before every build, including
`FusionTest` (bonding, the four-member limit, division, rest, determinism) and
`SharedWorldTest` (seeding, local sensing, colour transport). `tools/MergeProbe.java`
renders real Android Canvas frames offscreen and reports fusions, divisions, and
whether merged members have converged into one body.
