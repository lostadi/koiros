# Koiros 3.2

**Lee Ostadi’s evolving computational wallpaper — the dots now meet your apps.**

Download **Koiros-3.2.apk**, install it on Android 8.0 or newer, open Koiros,
and choose **Set wallpaper**. It holds both models: the **Emergent lattice**,
grown from a few rules every dot obeys (the default), and the **Classic forms**
with the koi, under **Model**. Upgrading keeps your saved controls.

## What is new since 1.8

- **3.0, the Emergent lattice:** dots that bond up to their valence, keep
  rhythm, gather, swirl and swim; bodies, fusion and division emerge.
- **3.1, shapes, heat and an open frame:** open lattices instead of collapsed
  clusters, colour as kind, heat, hidden dimensions, and groups arriving from
  beyond the screen's edges.
- **3.2, collisions with your apps:** with **Collide with my apps** and the
  optional *Koiros app collisions* reader turned on in Accessibility settings,
  dots bounce off your home screen's icons, folders, widgets and dock. The
  reader sees your launcher only and keeps only shapes. Koiros has no internet
  permission.
