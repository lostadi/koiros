## Inspiration

This type of algorithmic art, creatures drawn as clouds of points placed by short trigonometric formulas, was
created by digital artist [@yuruyurau](https://x.com/yuruyurau), whose "tweet-size" p5.js works are the original
inspiration for this app. The ten creature formulas in `lib/koi_algorithms.dart` (and their copy in
`LiveWallpaperService.kt`) are our own re-interpretations in that style: we changed the constants, terms and
structure, and adapted them to run as a live wallpaper.

## App icon

`assets/koi_clear_512.png`, `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/icon.png` and the generated launcher icons
under `android/app/src/main/res/` were made by Studio Coldstream (mathewmolt): the app's own creature art was
used as the basis and then interpreted with an AI image generator.
